\begin{abstract}
Robot-learning datasets can be locally valid and behaviorally wrong: every file loads, but a
command is replayed at the moment it was issued rather than when the motor executed it, the same
reconstructed scene appears in both training and testing, or an asset's collision role no longer
matches the state under evaluation. We call this \emph{silent state drift}. This paper presents
\emph{WorldEpisode}, an executable state-integrity contract for robot-learning datasets: five
machine-checkable invariants per episode (persistent identity, frame and clock mappings,
representation roles, temporal events, provenance) plus the two corpus-level obligations that carry
the headline results, lineage-disjoint evaluation splits and loss-explicit conversion.

On a public SO-101 LeRobot release, a random episode split overlaps every tested task--scene proxy
lineage. Across \ExpMlpSeedCount{} optimization seeds, mean episode nRMSE is
\ExpMlpRandomNrmse{} [\ExpMlpRandomNrmseCiLow, \ExpMlpRandomNrmseCiHigh]; holding out
\ExpSceneHeldoutGroupCount{} task--scene groups reduces measured proxy-lineage overlap to
\ExpSceneHeldoutOverlap{} and increases nRMSE to \ExpMlpHeldoutNrmse{}
[\ExpMlpHeldoutNrmseCiLow, \ExpMlpHeldoutNrmseCiHigh], a
\ExpMlpNrmseDifference{} increase [\ExpMlpNrmseDifferenceCiLow,
\ExpMlpNrmseDifferenceCiHigh]. Because task identity is part of the lineage key, this result does
not isolate scene leakage from task shift. A \ExpTimingDelayFrames-frame lag frozen on
\ExpTimingCalibrationEpisodes{} trajectories reduces held-out pooled action/state telemetry RMSE
from \ExpTimingZeroRmse{} to \ExpTimingFrozenRmse{} source position units on
\ExpTimingHeldoutEpisodes{} trajectories; the mean paired episode improvement is
\ExpTimingImprovement{} [\ExpTimingImprovementCiLow, \ExpTimingImprovementCiHigh]. This is a
telemetry proxy spanning \ExpTimingControllerConfigurationCount{} controller configuration and
without motor-effective timestamps. A complete-shard round-trip audit
over \ExpConversionScaleEpisodeCount{} public LeRobot episodes from
\ExpConversionScaleDatasetCount{} datasets preserves \ExpConversionScaleRowCount{} paired
state/action samples with maximum numerical error \ExpConversionScaleMaxError{} while reporting
source-absent semantics as conversion loss; the validator detects all \ExpFaultCaseCount{} injected
fault classes. We claim neither simulator-independent physics nor benchmark inflation without
faithful policy reruns.
\end{abstract}
