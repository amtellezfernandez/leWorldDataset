\section{Conclusion}

\emph{leWorldLayout} frames robot-world scene layout as a public, portable data contract. The format
records the authored world around a robot, declares frame and asset semantics, separates appearance
from physics, and provides a bridge from scene authoring to simulator transfer and scenario-level
evaluation. The immediate next step is to harden the v0.1 schema and report cross-simulator evidence
from the URDF Studio reference implementation.
