\section{Conclusion}

WorldEpisode reframes the opportunity away from ``LeRobot plus Gaussian splats in one file'' and
toward persistent, verifiable world--episode interoperability. Gaussian splats remain a high-value
profile, but the durable contribution is a storage-neutral contract for immutable world revisions,
persistent entity identity, frame and clock graphs, action semantics, representation roles, events,
provenance, uncertainty, loss-aware conversion, and lineage-safe evaluation. The immediate next step
is to harden the v0.1 schema, release a validator and golden conformance corpus, and demonstrate
independent bindings across existing robot-data and 3D-world systems.
