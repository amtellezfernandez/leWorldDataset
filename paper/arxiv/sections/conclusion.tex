\section{Conclusion}

WorldEpisode reframes the opportunity away from ``LeRobot plus Gaussian splats in one file'' and
toward persistent, verifiable world--episode interoperability. Gaussian splats remain a high-value
profile, but the durable contribution is a storage-neutral contract for immutable world revisions,
persistent entity identity, frame and clock graphs, action semantics, representation roles, events,
provenance, uncertainty, loss-aware conversion, and lineage-safe evaluation. The pilot implementation
turns those requirements into executable checks and reports measurable effects for conversion loss,
fault detection, replay timing, counterfactual augmentation, and leakage-safe splitting. The next
step is to replace the synthetic pilots with real multi-robot datasets and independently maintained
bindings.
