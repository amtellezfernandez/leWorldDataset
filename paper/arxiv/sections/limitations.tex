\section{Limitations and Open Gates}
\label{sec:limits}

The current evidence is deliberately scoped: it exposes hidden failure mechanisms rather than
claiming full robot-learning, game-engine, or autonomous-driving generality. The boundary of every
empirical claim is tabulated in Appendix~\ref{app:boundaries}, and each open gate's commands and
acceptance rules are boxed in Appendix~\ref{app:gates}; this section states what matters most.

\paragraph{Beyond robotics (future work).}
All measured evidence in this paper is the WorldEpisode robotics profile; the broader Universal
Spatial State framing is proposed, not demonstrated outside robotics. \ExpNonrobotPilotCount{}
deterministic pilots only
illustrate that the same vocabulary can express non-robotics drift. In a game-engine collision pilot,
a client loads a stale arena collision asset after an authoritative patch, which the asset-digest,
state-revision, and role checks catch. In an autonomous-driving clock pilot, camera and lidar logs
deserialize correctly, but a \ExpAvClockOffsetMilliseconds{} ms undeclared clock offset at
\ExpAvSpeedMetersPerSecond{} m/s creates a \ExpAvFusionErrorMeters{} m fusion error against a
\ExpAvToleranceMeters{} m tolerance, which the declared clock mapping corrects to
\ExpAvCorrectedErrorMeters{} m. These are
illustrative pilots, not production game-engine or AV benchmark results. A stronger paper should add
at least one public non-robotics corpus and an independently maintained adapter.

\paragraph{Principal limitations.}
The headline holdout is confounded: task identity is part of the proxy-lineage key, so it measures a
task--scene shift and cannot attribute the change to scene overlap alone. The MLP uses
\ExpMlpSeedCount{} optimization seeds and task one-hot features; crossed bootstrap intervals capture
seed and held-out-episode variation, but \ExpMlpSeedCount{} seeds remain a small training sample and
the \ExpOfflineSuccessThreshold{} nRMSE threshold is reported only as a secondary diagnostic rather
than task success. ACT/Diffusion training and rollout evidence remain open; the pinned compatibility
probe now completes \ExpPolicyVisionTrainingStepCount{}/\ExpPolicyVisionProbeCount{}
smoke-training paths after digest-pinned source-image materialization, but it produces no trained
checkpoint, held-out policy metric, or rollout. The timing audit calibrates on
\ExpTimingCalibrationEpisodes{} trajectories and evaluates \ExpTimingHeldoutEpisodes{} held-out
trajectories, but it has no motor-effective timestamps and covers
\ExpTimingRobotTypeCount{} robot type and \ExpTimingControllerConfigurationCount{} controller
configuration. MuJoCo and Genesis still execute the same minimal servo model on
\ExpReplayTraceCount{} \ExpReplayTraceNoun{} for the timing result. The separate contact-rich audit
covers \ExpContactReplayTaskCount{} scripted primitive tasks rather than an articulated robot or
hardware reference; despite \ExpContactReplayOutcomeAgreement{} task-outcome agreement, its
\ExpContactReplayFinalOrientationDeg$^\circ$ aggregate final-orientation error rules out equivalent
physics. Conversion
covers complete selected Parquet shards from
\ExpConversionScaleDatasetCount{} datasets, not their full corpora or source video payloads. Injected faults establish
detection on known mutations, not natural prevalence or false-positive rates in deployment.

The implementation is also single-sourced. The clean-room reader is internal, the package is not
yet an upstream LeRobot/Rerun integration, and no external compatible dataset is available. The
\ExpNaturalDatasetCount-dataset natural-source audit mixes \ExpNaturalActiveDatasetCount{} active
artifacts with \ExpNaturalSourceLevelDatasetCount{} source-level metadata reviews and has no
maintainer adjudication.

\paragraph{Societal impact.}
Fail-closed state checks may reduce unsafe or irreproducible robot evaluations and wasted training
compute. Conversely, an incomplete contract can create false assurance, provenance fields can expose
sensitive capture locations or operators, and content-addressed lineage can aid dataset linkage.
Deployments should minimize or access-control provenance, retain native safety interlocks, and treat
conformance as an audit aid rather than a safety certificate.
