\section{Open Contributions (colleague working notes)}
\label{sec:open-contributions}

This appendix is a coordination surface, not a paper result. It renders only in the working
build (the \texttt{\textbackslash collabtrue} switch in \path{main.tex}); flip that switch to
\texttt{\textbackslash collabfalse} to remove it before any external submission. Each card below is
a claimable unit of work with a fixed anchor: \textbf{Where} it lives in this paper and the repo,
\textbf{What is missing}, \textbf{How to close it} (exact commands), and \textbf{Needs} (environment
and access). The purple \collabmark{Cx} tags in the Evidence and Limitations sections
point back to these cards.

\paragraph{How to claim a card.} Put your name in the \textbf{Owner} slot, open an RFC issue for any
normative change (per \path{GOVERNANCE.md}), and keep published requirement IDs stable. The four
experiment gates (C1--C4) are the same ones indexed in
\path{docs/experiments/open_reproduction_gates/open_reproduction_gates.json}.

\paragraph{Finishing a card also means finishing the prose.} The paper text is complete but
evidence-gated: closing a card is not done until you (1) fold the measured numbers into the cited
section, (2) update the evidence-boundary table in the Limitations section and the summary figure in
the Failure Case Studies section, and (3) keep the gates green by re-running
\texttt{python3 tools/paper\_claim\_audit.py -{}-strict} and
\texttt{python3 tools/artifact\_freshness.py -{}-strict}. The whole readiness suite is
\texttt{make readiness}.

\collabcard{C1}{ACT/Diffusion policy metrics and rollout impact}
{Evidence, Case 1 (scene-lineage leakage); Limitations, ``ACT/Diffusion and rollout impact''
open-gate box; artifacts under \path{docs/experiments/lerobot_policy_gate/}. Gate \texttt{POLICY.ROLL.001}.}
{ACT and Diffusion Policy offline metrics on \emph{both} the random and scene-disjoint splits, plus at
least one simulator or physical rollout on the same split manifest. Source videos must be mirrored

{\texttt{python3 tools/lerobot\_policy\_leakage\_gate.py}, then
\texttt{bash docs/experiments/lerobot\_policy\_gate/run\_lerobot\_policy\_jobs.sh} in a LeRobot
training environment. The compact physical split packages under
\path{docs/experiments/lerobot_policy_gate/physical_splits/} are already committed, so offline

\collabcard{C2}{Famous-benchmark score-inflation proof}
{Evidence, Case 7 (famous benchmarks); Limitations, ``famous-benchmark score-inflation proof''
open-gate box. Gate \texttt{BENCH.INFLATE.001}.}
{A benchmark-specific WorldEpisode conversion, a faithful published-protocol policy rerun, and a
paired corrected evaluation showing a measured baseline-minus-corrected score drop. The committed

{\texttt{uv run -{}-with pyarrow -{}-with requests -{}-with numpy python
tools/famous\_benchmark\_policy\_rerun.py -{}-benchmark droid\_100 -{}-required}, then
\texttt{python3 tools/benchmark\_inflation\_gate.py -{}-required}. Offline: pre-place the pinned
Parquet shards under \texttt{-{}-cache-root <dir>} at the repo-id/revision path the tool prints, then
re-run with that flag to skip all network access (the runner also attempts a DNS-over-HTTPS fallback
automatically).}
{Reliable network access to Hugging Face; the published policy checkpoint and evaluation protocol for
the target benchmark; compute for the rerun.}
